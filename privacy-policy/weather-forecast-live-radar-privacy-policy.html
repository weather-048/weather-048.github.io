1. Information We May Collect

The types of information processed may include the following, depending on how you use the app.

If you grant location permission, the app may use your device's approximate or precise location, as permitted by your device settings, to identify local weather conditions, forecasts, radar information, air-quality data, and relevant weather alerts. Location access is optional. If you deny or disable it, you may still be able to search for or select a city manually, if that feature is available.

Saved cities or locations you choose may be stored on your device and/or processed by the app's service providers to retrieve weather information. The exact storage and processing depend on the app's implementation.

Weather Requests and Location Preferences

When you request weather information, the app may process a city name, geographic coordinates, language, units, or other settings needed to return current conditions, hourly and 7-day forecasts, precipitation and cloud data, severe weather alerts, air-quality readings, UV index, wind, visibility, sunrise and sunset, or moon-phase information.

Device, Diagnostic, and Usage Information

The app or third-party services may automatically receive technical information such as device model, operating system and app version, language and region settings, IP address, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, crash reports, diagnostic logs, feature interactions, timestamps, and performance data. This information may be used to maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services. The actual information collected depends on the SDKs and configuration used in the released app.

Information You Provide

If you contact support or send feedback, we may process the details you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and any information you include to help us respond.
